About the three suggestions

Generated when AI Hub loads or the organization/model changes, using your selected context switches. The chosen context window also applies.

Always: the selected organization’s name and an instruction to return three short questions. Overview: visible submission count and item-selection count/total. Profile: purpose, description, category and saved business details. Documents: up to three matching excerpts.

Suggestions are temporary. They receive no earlier chat, saved memory, personal profile, attachments, full form schemas, member directory or dataset records. With all context switches off, only the organization name and question-generation instruction are sent.
